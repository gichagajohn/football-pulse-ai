CUSTOM FONTS
============

Drop your .ttf font files here to use them in poster generation.

Recommended free fonts for a premium sports-media look:
- Bebas Neue Bold    → https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bebas+Neue
- Oswald Bold        → https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Oswald
- Anton              → https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Anton

After downloading, rename them:
  bold.ttf     → your bold display font
  regular.ttf  → your regular body font

Then update settings.py FONTS_DIR paths if needed.

Without custom fonts, the system falls back to DejaVu Sans (system font),
which is already installed on Ubuntu and works fine.
